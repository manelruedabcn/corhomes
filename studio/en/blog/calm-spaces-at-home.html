Calm spaces at home: how to create a corner to unwind

Updated 26 November 2026 · 3 min read

You don't need a whole room to have a place of calm. Sometimes a corner by a window, a bench under the stairs or a bedroom that finally stops being a storeroom is enough. What matters is that the space is designed for one thing only: slowing down.

Where to put it

Look for a spot away from circulation and from the noisy areas of the house, such as the kitchen or the entrance. Good options:

  • A corner by a window with good light and a view, even if only of a patio.
  • A space under the stairs or a wide hallway.
  • Part of the bedroom separated by a low shelf or a curtain.
  • A covered terrace or a porch sheltered from the wind.
  • A small room that has no clear use today.

Soft, dimmable light

Daylight is the best ally, filtered through linen curtains or blinds that soften it. For the afternoon and evening, a floor or reading lamp with warm light, 2700 K or lower, and a dimmer. Avoid direct spotlights from the ceiling: they create harsh shadows and feel more like work than rest.

Quiet

A calm space needs protecting from noise. If it sits next to a shared wall or a noisy area, the wall can be reinforced with acoustic insulation. Rugs, thick curtains, cushions and shelves full of books absorb sound and make the room feel more enclosed. There are more ideas in home acoustics.

Materials that invite touch

Wood, linen, wool, lime, cork or terracotta bring warmth and texture. In a calm space, steer clear of shiny surfaces and intense colours. Mediterranean style offers a perfect palette: off-whites, sands and soft greens.

A built-in bench or bespoke seat

A long bench under a window, with a good cushion and drawers below, is one of the most rewarding elements of a renovation. It takes up little room, uses dead space and creates a natural place to sit and read or look outside.

Storage so it doesn't fill up

The enemy of a calm corner is ending up as the place where everything gets dropped. Build closed storage nearby so things have their place and the space stays clear.

No screens

If you can, make it a place without a television or computer. A discreet socket for a lamp is enough. The idea is that the space becomes associated with something else: reading, listening to music, meditating, stretching or simply doing nothing.

A pleasant temperature

Corners by windows can be cold in winter and hot in summer if the frame isn't good. Check that window and its insulation; sometimes replacing it is what turns an uncomfortable corner into the favourite spot in the house. We explain it in energy-efficient windows.

A touch of green

One or two plants that tolerate the light conditions of the corner add life without needing much care. If there is a patio or terrace, framing that view from the seat is even better.

Ideas for the space you have

  • Two square metres: armchair, floor lamp, side table and a low shelf.
  • A window recess: built-in bench with drawers, cushion and a linen curtain.
  • A small room: warm floor, a large rug, a low seat, indirect light and nothing else.
  • A terrace: awning or pergola, outdoor curtains, an outdoor sofa and plants that give shade.
